Practice order

Tap the card or button, or press Space / Enter.

62 cards: 18 vowels, 41 consonants, the signs ං and ඃ, and the combination ක්ෂ. Rare letters are included.

Ordered starts with අ and loops. Random shuffles the full set before repeating.

The large Latin text is the letter’s reading; the smaller line gives its name. Consonants include the inherent vowel a. ā, ǣ, ī, ū, ē, ō are long vowels; æ is like a in “cat”; c is like ch in “chair”.

Dots distinguish retroflex sounds (ṭ, ḍ, ṇ, ḷ); ṛ / ṝ and ḷ / ḹ on vowel cards are vocalic r / l. A breve marks prenasalization (n̆g, n̆d, m̆b). kh, gh, ch, jh, ṭh, ḍh, th, dh, ph and bh denote aspiration; modern readings may coincide. ś and ṣ are sh sounds; v is often pronounced like w.

Alphabet checked against SLS 1134. Combining vowel marks (පිල්ලම්) are separate from the independent alphabet letters.
